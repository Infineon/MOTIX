VCPH monitoring

To assure enough supply voltage to drive the output stages, the bridge driver includes a VCPH pin undervoltage monitoring function. The differential voltage between pin VCPH and VSCP is monitored for undervoltage. The undervoltage detection threshold of VPCH pin is configurable by control bit CP_UV_TH. Failure reaction of VCPH undervoltage detection is not configurable and fixed to latched error 1 (LE1).

Table 1. Settings
Setting Nominal configured VCPH undervoltage threshold Comments
CP_UV_TH = 0 8.5 V Typical use case
CP_UV_TH = 1 7.5 V Recommended for low VSCP supply (low supply configuration)

To avoid too much overdrive supply voltage at VCPH pin, the device includes as well an over-voltage comparator for VCPH supply monitoring. The VCPH voltage is monitoring against a VCPH overdrive target voltage threshold and is used for the charge pump clock gating scheme. In case the differential voltage VCPH - VSCP is above the VCPH overdrive target voltage threshold (approx. 15 V), the charge pump clock is stopped for the second charge pump stage and is restarted when VCPH is detected below VCPH overdrive target voltage threshold. No additional failure reaction is triggered and no SPI status bit is set.

The correct function of both VCPH undervoltage and VCPH overvoltage comparators can be tested by dedicated charge pump monitoring self-test.
