Interpreting the results of the off-state diagnostics

In normal operation mode, during the off-state diagnostic check (provided HBx_EN = 1, CP_EN = 1, OFF_DIAG_EN = 1), the level of the output voltage SHx can be read via the status bits VSHx_L and VSHx_H:

  • VSHx_L = 0B AND VSHx_H = 0B : VSHx low (VSHx < VSHL)
  • VSHx_L = 1B AND VSHx_H = 1B: VSHx high (VSHx > VSHH)

Based on the HBxIDIAG ​settings (activation of the pull-down currents of LSx MOSFETs) the SHx level can be controlled to high or low and read-out by the microcontroller via VSHx_H and VSHx_L status bits. Depending on the read-out, the following failures can be differentiated:

  • MOSFET short circuit to GND
  • MOSFET short circuit to VSCP
  • Open load (disconnected motor)​

A sequence of four configurations has to be performed by the microcontroller via SPI for the pull-down currents. The results are also interpreted strictly by the microcontroller:

  • Configuration 1: HB1IDIAG, HB2IDIAG, HB3IDIAG are all disabled
  • Configuration 2: HB1IDIAG, HB2IDIAG are enabled while HB3IDIAG is disabled
  • Configuration 3: HB2IDIAG, HB3IDIAG are enabled while HB1IDIAG is disabled
  • Configuration 4: HB1IDIAG, HB3IDIAG are enabled while HB2IDIAG is disabled

The phase pins SHx are monitored by the two comparators with thresholds VSHL and VSHH.

The SHx level is indicated via the status bits VSHx_L and depending on the pull-down current configuration, (enabled or not ) the correct functionality of SHx pins can be detected.

During this test, the target is to enable 2 pull-down currents at a time and check if the SHx can be pulled low or not by this internal current. By design, it ensures that 2 x IPDDIAG current is always stronger (higher) than 3 x IPUDIAG current (which is always ON - the total current of 3 HS gate drivers in active discharge phase).
