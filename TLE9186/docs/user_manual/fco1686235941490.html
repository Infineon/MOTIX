Optional components explanation

The external components listed below are not required for the correct operation of the device. They are optional for the device but might be required by the application. It is the system integrator's responsibility to assess whether the external component is required by the application and to select appropriate optional component values.

Table 1. Optional external components
Name Description Further explanation
CVIOf VIO capacitor filter capacitor For improved electromagnetic emission results it is recommended to place CVIOf capacitor very close to the VIO pin (pin 9) and direct connection to GND pin (pin 4). Furthermore it is recommended to connect GND (pin 4) directly to E-Pad. E-pad connected to PCB GND layer.
RSPIx, RIHx, RILx, RERR Series resistor microcontroller Recommended if the device is to be operated outside specified absolute maximum ratings of the device, such as GND shift between device and over external IC. Series resistors can be used to protect external ICs from high voltage due to internal failure propagation in the device
RGSx Gate-source resistor Recommended in case of open gate pin failure on PCB and PCB open pin failure cannot be excluded
RSHx Phase source resistor Recommended in case device operation is outside specified absolute maximum ratings of device
CSHx Phase source capacitor May improve electromagnetic immunity
DSHx Phase source diode Recommended if the device is to be operated outside specified absolute maximum ratings of device
CSLx Source low-side FET capacitor Recommended for improved electromagnetic immunity
DCSI Current sense amplifier protection diode Optional component to protect the current sense amplifier inputs pins against damage due to absolute maximum ratings violation
RSNx Snubber resistor The snubber circuit is optional only. If snubber circuit is mounted the value of the snubber resistor may vary from 1 to 10 Ω (nominal values) and the snubber capacitor may vary from 0.1 to 22 nF (nominal values)
CSNx Snubber capacitor
CBridge1 VBridge ELCO capacitor per half-bridge –
CBridge2 VBridge ceramic capacitor per half-bridge –
DISPx Current sense amplifier protection diode This optional components is needed to protect the current sense amplifier inputs pins against damage due to AMR violation caused by solder joint issues at the external shunt resistor RSHUNT

Since the ERR_N pin of the device is an open-drain output, an external pull-up resistor RERR_PU to microcontroller supply VIO is needed to pull ERR_N signal "high". The optional series resistor RERR protects the microcontroller from damage in case the pull-up resistor RERR_PU is shorted. Dimensioning depends on supply and the maximum current capability of the microcontroller.

Series resistors at the serial peripheral interface (SPI) and IHx/ILx PWM input pins can limit the maximum switching frequency. Additionally the voltage divider created by external series resistor and internal device pull-down or pull-up resistors have to be considered to ensure correct logic "low" and "high" levels.
